## Open Source DevOps Stack

I believe in the power of open-source technologies. With free and open-source tools, anyone can get started building modern, production-ready infrastructure. The open-source DevOps ecosystem provides everything needed to run reliable, scalable systems.

### Kubernetes - The Foundation

Kubernetes ties everything together as the central platform for container orchestration and cloud-native applications. What makes Kubernetes a safe choice for your infrastructure is that it's backed by the **Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF)**, a vendor-neutral non-profit organization under the Linux Foundation. This ensures long-term stability, community-driven development, and protection against vendor lock-in.

### Observability Matters

Modern infrastructure requires comprehensive observability. I work extensively with the Grafana stack (Prometheus, Loki, Tempo, Mimir) and OpenTelemetry to provide:
- **Metrics** - Understanding system performance and resource usage
- **Logs** - Debugging and troubleshooting applications
- **Traces** - Following requests through distributed systems

### Platform Engineering - The Next Step

Platform engineering is the natural evolution of the DevOps journey. While DevOps focuses on breaking down silos between development and operations, platform engineering takes it further by building internal developer platforms that enable teams to self-serve infrastructure and deployments. This reduces cognitive load on developers while maintaining operational excellence.
